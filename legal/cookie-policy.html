Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

cookie 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

category 2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on Owl Assist for the following reasons:

  • Essential & Authentication Cookies: These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our platform and to use some of its features, such as accessing secure dashboard areas via Clerk auth.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: These cookies collect information that is used in aggregate form to help us understand how our website is being used (e.g., tracking country locations to serve pricing) and to measure how effective our marketing campaigns are.
  • AI Widget Context: Functional cookies are used by the AI widget to remember user sessions, conversation states, and booking progression without prompting authentication.

tune 3. Controlling Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies.

If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to some functionality and areas of our website (like log-in dashboards and saved chat histories in the booking widget) may be restricted.

smart_toy 2. Artificial Intelligence and LLM Service Integration

Our platform integrates advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to facilitate receptionist conversations, schedule bookings, and query your business FAQs.

Specifically, we leverage models and APIs provided by:

  • Google Gemini: Serving as our primary language model API provider.
  • Mistral AI: Supporting fallback structures and specific localized intent categorization.
  • Groq: Powering ultra-low latency open models (e.g., Llama 3, Mixtral) to ensure rapid booking confirmation flows.
  • OpenRouter: Providing robust API routing and multi-provider models fallback capabilities.

Note: By utilizing our AI receptionist services, you acknowledge that AI models generate content based on parameters and data provided by your business. While we train and optimize models for reliability, Owl Assist is not liable for minor hallucinations, conversational errors, or formatting anomalies caused by these third-party AI APIs.
